**A HYPOTHESIS THE MODEL CAN ANSWER**

Your hypothesis says the forward will give the most predictable dollar proceeds and the put will do better "if the euro strengthens enough to make up for the premium". Read it as the CFO would: how much is enough? As written, there is no result your Stage 3 model could produce that would disagree with it, and a hypothesis nothing can disagree with does not tell anyone which hedge to pick.

A number changes that. You already have the pieces in the brief, the 1.1705 forward and the $102,820 premium. At what euro rate in 182 days does the put start beating the forward, once that premium is paid? Work it out yourself and put it in the hypothesis. Then the falsifier follows from it: what result in your model would show the hypothesis wrong, something that could come out either way? "If the option does not perform better under those conditions" cannot fail, because it repeats the condition.
